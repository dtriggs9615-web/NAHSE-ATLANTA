About NAHSE

Our Mission

The National Association of Health Services Executives (N.A.H.S.E.) is a non-profit association of Black health care executives founded in 1968. We are committed to promoting the advancement and development of Black health care leaders and elevating the quality of health care services rendered to minority and underserved communities. NAHSE's purpose is to ensure greater participation of minority groups in the health field.

Our Vision

N.A.H.S.E. will be recognized globally as the premier professional membership society for Blacks in health care management. We strive to improve the health status, economic opportunities and educational advancement of the communities we serve through professional programs, workshops, educational initiatives, mentoring, scholarships, and community service projects.

Strategic Pillars

Visibility

Elevating the presence and voice of Black healthcare leaders in our industry and communities

Values

Upholding integrity, excellence, and commitment to health equity in all we do

Viability

Ensuring sustainable growth and long-term impact of our organization
